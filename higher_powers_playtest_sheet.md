# Higher Powers — One-page playtest sheet
*Working prototype · 3 rounds · Standard 106-tile Rummikub set*

**Premise.** Twelve ambitious angels organize numbered souls into Heaven's Grand Order. God changes the rules each round. Deliver your whole hand first; undelivered souls count against their keepers.

## Setup and turn
- Use standard Rummikub tiles (1–13, four colors, two of each, two jokers). Deal **14 souls per player**. Choose one angel per player; each power is **once per round**.
- **No 30-point opening requirement.** From the first turn, play any legal meld and rearrange the shared table. Finish every turn with all of Heaven in legal formations. If you don't play, draw one soul; use the Divine Favor draw selection below.
- **Choir:** 3–4 same-rank souls, all different colors. **Procession:** 3+ consecutive ranks of one color. Active decrees override these definitions. Standard Rummikub joker rules apply unless a decree says otherwise.
- **Divine Favor:** Reset each round. Advance by the **net number of souls added to Heaven on your turn** (not by rearranging or swapping). Rank contributors: with N players, the lowest may inspect 1 bag tile and choose it, the next 2, and so on up to N; return unchosen tiles. **Ties need a playtest ruling.**
- A round ends when a player empties their hand. Score as standard Rummikub: each other player loses the face value of souls remaining; the winner gains the sum of those losses. Highest cumulative score after three rounds wins.

## Angel powers (one use each round)
**Heaven / rearrangement**
1. **Zadkiel — Forgiveness:** Take one soul from Heaven into your hand; leave legal melds.
2. **Raphael — Penance:** Put one soul from Heaven into your private Purgatory. While occupied, only attempt to place it into a procession or draw.
3. **Selaphiel — Choir Sanctuary:** Create Sanctuary with one complete choir. Only you may extend or split its protected formations; separate new formations cannot enter.
4. **Barachiel — Procession Sanctuary:** As above, for a procession.
5. **Chamuel — Communion:** Look at an opponent's entire hand and choose one soul; play it into Heaven along with at least one from your own hand, or return it.
6. **Jophiel — Transfiguration:** Take exactly two souls from Heaven into your hand while contributing at least two from your hand in the same turn; final table legal.

**Hand / draw / tempo**
7. **Uriel — Foretelling:** On a draw, inspect 8 bag souls, take 1, return the rest.
8. **Jegudiel — Zeal:** After your turn, immediately take another full turn.
9. **Raguel — Judgment:** Return any number of hand souls to the bag; blindly draw twice as many.
10. **Haniel — Heavenly Dance:** Choose X up to the smallest hand size; everyone simultaneously passes X chosen souls right.
11. **Gabriel — Calling:** Name a rank; all opponents give you all souls of that rank.
12. **Michael — Banishment:** Give all your souls of one rank to one opponent; may empty your hand.

## Round decrees
**Rounds 1 and 2:** Shuffle the 10 ordinary decrees; reveal one each round **without replacement**.
1. **Even/Odd:** Processions step by 2, same color.
2. **All Are Welcome:** Choirs may repeat colors.
3. **Colorful Processions:** Consecutive ranks, any colors.
4. **Candy Cane:** Processions alternate exactly two colors.
5. **Eternal Circle:** Processions may wrap 13 → 1.
6. **God's Poker Night:** Also allow two pairs (2+2) and full houses (3+2), with different colors within each pair or triple.
7. **Grand Procession:** Minimum procession length 4.
8. **Stairway to Heaven:** Procession rank steps may each be 1 or 2.
9. **Heavenly Pairs:** Two different-colored same-rank souls make a choir.
10. **Mirror Choir:** Complementary ranks summing to 14 (*exact formation definition TBD*).

**Round 3:** Shuffle five catch-up decrees and reveal one:
- **Divine Retribution:** If someone other than the overall score leader (“God's Favorite”) goes out, double the Favorite's penalty.
- **Shared Sins:** Secretly choose another player; receive a credit equal to their end-of-round penalty.
- **Scales of Justice:** Commit up to five souls at round start; delivered ones earn face-value credits, those still held count triple.
- **Favor Head Start:** Favor resets; score leader starts at 0, second at 1, third at 2, etc.
- **Redemption:** Round winner erases their largest negative score from rounds 1–2.

**Playtest rulings to settle:** Favor ties; protected formation release/replacement; joker contribution accounting; Purgatory end-of-round penalty; exact Mirror Choir pattern; handling catch-up credits and winner score. Keep special zones physically visible; no hidden turn timers.
