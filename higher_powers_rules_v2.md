# Higher Powers
## Playtest Rules — Version 0.2

*A game of divine organization, ambitious angels, and heavenly bureaucracy.*

**Players:** 3–5 (provisional)  
**Rounds:** 3  
**Components:** Standard Rummikub set, 12 Angel cards, 10 Heavenly Decrees, 5 Final Judgment cards, Divine Favor tracks and markers.

### 1. The Story

Heaven is overflowing with newly arrived souls, and God has appointed twelve angels to restore order.

The angels organize souls into Choirs and Processions, earning Divine Favor for their work. Unfortunately, each angel has their own miraculous abilities, and God keeps issuing new decrees about how Heaven should be organized.

The angels are supposed to cooperate, but only one can become God's Favorite.

### 2. Objective

The objective is to finish the three rounds with the highest cumulative score.

In each round, angels compete to deliver all the souls in their hands into Heaven.

The first angel to empty their hand ends the round. Other angels receive penalties for the souls they have failed to deliver.

### 3. Components and Setup

Use a standard 106-tile Rummikub set:

- Four colors, with ranks 1 through 13.
- Two copies of each numbered tile in each color.
- Two Jokers.

Each player receives 14 randomly drawn souls.

Place the remaining souls face down in a bag.

Each player receives one Angel card, which grants a unique power usable once per round.

Each player also receives a Divine Favor marker, initially placed at zero.

At the beginning of each round, reveal the applicable Heavenly Decree.

The shared playing area is called **Heaven**, or the **Grand Order**.

### 4. Organizing Heaven

Souls are organized into two types of formations.

**Choirs**

A Choir consists of three or four souls of the same rank, each of a different color.

Example: Red 7, Blue 7, Black 7.

**Processions**

A Procession consists of three or more souls of consecutive ranks, all of the same color.

Example: Blue 4, Blue 5, Blue 6, Blue 7.

The active Heavenly Decree may change these definitions.

#### Rearranging Heaven

On their turn, an angel may freely rearrange the souls already in Heaven.

They may split formations, combine formations, and move souls between Choirs and Processions.

They may also contribute any number of souls from their hand.

**At the end of every turn, all souls in Heaven must belong to legal formations.**

There is no 30-point opening requirement. Angels may begin contributing and rearranging immediately.

A player may not ordinarily take souls from Heaven back into their hand. Certain Angel powers explicitly permit this.

### 5. Taking a Turn

Play proceeds clockwise.

On your turn, you may choose one of two ordinary actions:

**Organize Heaven**

Play one or more souls from your hand into Heaven, rearranging existing formations as necessary.

All formations must be legal when you finish.

**Draw a Soul**

Instead of playing, draw one soul using the Divine Favor drawing procedure.

You may choose to draw even when a legal play is available.

Drawing ends your turn.

You may also activate your Angel power at the time specified by that power.

### 6. Divine Favor

Divine Favor rewards angels who contribute souls to Heaven.

Each angel has a visible Divine Favor track, which resets to zero at the beginning of each round.

#### Earning Favor

Whenever you add souls to Heaven, advance your track by the net number of souls you contribute during that turn.

Examples:

- Add three souls from your hand: gain 3 Favor.
- Rearrange existing formations without adding souls: gain 0 Favor.
- Add four souls while taking one soul into your hand using Forgiveness: gain 3 Favor.
- Exchange two souls from your hand with two from Heaven: gain 0 Favor.

Only net additions count. Replaying previously retrieved souls must not allow an angel to manufacture additional Favor.

#### Drawing with Divine Favor

Divine Favor determines how many souls you may examine when drawing from the bag.

Rank all angels by their current Divine Favor totals.

The angel with the lowest Favor examines one soul. The next examines two, and so forth.

The highest-ranked angel in an N-player game examines N souls.

| Rank | 3 players | 4 players | 5 players |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Examine 3 | Examine 4 | Examine 5 |
| 2nd | Examine 2 | Examine 3 | Examine 4 |
| 3rd | Examine 1 | Examine 2 | Examine 3 |
| 4th | — | Examine 1 | Examine 2 |
| 5th | — | — | Examine 1 |

When drawing:

1. Randomly take the indicated number of souls from the bag.
2. Examine them privately.
3. Choose exactly one soul to keep.
4. Return the others to the bag.

The ranking is recalculated whenever a player draws.

**Provisional tie rule:** Tied angels receive the drawing privilege of the highest position occupied by their tied group.

For example, if two angels tie for first in a four-player game, both examine four souls.

### 7. The Twelve Angels

Each angel has one unique power.

Unless otherwise specified, an Angel power may be activated once per round.

Used powers should be indicated by turning the Angel card sideways.

#### Angels of Heavenly Organization

**Zadkiel — Forgiveness**

Once per round, take one soul from Heaven into your hand.

You may rearrange Heaven and contribute additional souls during the same turn.

All formations must be legal when your turn ends.

**Raphael — Penance**

Once per round, remove one soul from Heaven and place it in your private Purgatory.

While your Purgatory contains a soul, you may not play other souls from your hand.

On subsequent turns, you may attempt to rescue the soul by incorporating it into a legal Procession, rearranging Heaven as necessary.

If you cannot rescue it, you may draw normally.

Your Purgatory remains occupied until the soul is rescued.

**Selaphiel — Sanctuary of Choirs**

Once per round, place one complete Choir into your private Sanctuary.

Only you may rearrange or retrieve souls from that Sanctuary.

Create the Sanctuary with exactly one Choir. Later, extend and split protected formations; entirely new formations cannot be added. All protected Choirs must remain legal.

**Barachiel — Sanctuary of Processions**

Once per round, place one complete Procession into your private Sanctuary.

Only you may rearrange or retrieve souls from that Sanctuary.

Create the Sanctuary with exactly one Procession. Later, extend and split protected formations; entirely new formations cannot be added. All protected Processions must remain legal.

**Chamuel — Communion**

Once per round, look at a chosen opponent's entire hand and choose one soul.

You may take that soul and deliver it into Heaven, provided you also contribute at least one soul from your own hand during the same turn.

If you cannot or choose not to use it, return the chosen soul to its owner. Looking at the hand spends the power, even if no soul is played.

You receive Divine Favor credit for the net souls added to Heaven.

**Jophiel — Transfiguration**

Once per round, take exactly two souls from Heaven into your hand.

During the same turn, contribute at least two souls from your hand to Heaven.

You may freely rearrange Heaven and contribute additional souls.

Heaven must be legal when your turn ends.

#### Angels of Providence and Influence

**Uriel — Foretelling**

Once per round, when drawing, examine eight randomly selected souls from the bag.

Choose one to keep and return the others.

This replaces your ordinary Divine Favor drawing privilege for that draw.

**Jegudiel — Zeal**

Once per round, after completing your turn, immediately take one additional full turn.

Play then passes to the next angel in the normal order.

**Raguel — Judgment**

Once per round, choose any positive number of souls from your hand.

Return them to the bag, then draw twice that number of souls blindly.

All returned souls enter the bag before the new souls are drawn.

**Haniel — The Heavenly Dance**

Once per round, choose a number X, no greater than the smallest hand size among the players.

Every angel secretly selects exactly X souls from their hand.

All angels simultaneously pass their selected souls to the player on their right.

Players may not inspect incoming souls before choosing what to pass.

**Gabriel — Calling**

Once per round, announce a rank.

Every other angel must give you all souls of that rank from their hand.

You may call a rank even if you do not hold it yourself.

**Michael — Banishment**

Once per round, choose a rank represented in your hand and select one opponent.

Give that opponent every soul of the chosen rank from your hand.

This power may empty your hand and end the round.

### 8. Heavenly Decrees

Each round has one Heavenly Decree, revealed before the round begins.

The decree changes the rules for organizing Heaven or scoring the round.

#### Rounds One and Two: Ordinary Decrees

Shuffle the ten ordinary Heavenly Decrees.

Draw one for the first round and another for the second round, without replacement.

**1. Even and Odd**

Processions consist of consecutive even numbers or consecutive odd numbers, advancing by two.

Example: Red 2-4-6-8.

**2. All Are Welcome**

Choirs may contain multiple souls of the same color, provided all souls share the same rank.

**3. Colorful Processions**

Processions may contain any combination of colors, provided the ranks are consecutive.

**4. Candy Cane**

Processions must alternate between exactly two colors.

Example: Red 4, Blue 5, Red 6, Blue 7.

**5. The Eternal Circle**

Processions may wrap from 13 back to 1.

Example: Blue 12-13-1-2.

**6. God's Poker Night**

In addition to ordinary Choirs and Processions, angels may create:

- Two Pair: exactly two souls of one rank and two of another.
- Full House: exactly three souls of one rank and two of another.

Each pair must use two different colors, and a Full House’s triple must use three different colors. Colors may repeat between different ranks.

**7. The Grand Procession**

Processions require at least four souls instead of three.

**8. Stairway to Heaven**

Processions may advance by either one or two ranks at each step.

Example: Red 3-5-6-8.

**9. Heavenly Pairs**

Choirs may consist of two souls of the same rank and different colors.

**10. Mirror Choir**

Special Choirs may use any two ranks whose values sum to 14 and whose colours match.

### 9. The Final Judgment

The third and final round uses a separate deck of five catch-up decrees.

Shuffle the Final Judgment deck and reveal one decree.

God's Favorite is the angel with the highest cumulative score after the first two rounds.

These decrees create opportunities for trailing angels to recover, without guaranteeing anyone an advantage.

**1. Divine Retribution**

If an angel other than God's Favorite goes out, God's Favorite suffers double their normal end-of-round penalty.

If God's Favorite goes out, scoring is normal.

**2. Shared Sins**

Before the round begins, each angel secretly selects another angel.

At the end of the round, receive a scoring credit equal to the penalty incurred by your selected angel.

Multiple angels may select the same player.

**3. The Scales of Justice**

At the beginning of the round, each angel may commit up to five souls from their hand.

Each committed soul successfully delivered into Heaven earns a scoring credit equal to its face value.

Each committed soul still in the player's hand at the end of the round incurs triple its normal penalty.

Committed souls must remain identifiable.

**4. Divine Favor Head Start**

Divine Favor resets normally.

Before play begins, advance each angel's Divine Favor marker according to their position in the cumulative standings:

- First place: 0 Favor.
- Second place: 1 Favor.
- Third place: 2 Favor.
- Fourth place: 3 Favor.
- Fifth place: 4 Favor.

All subsequent Favor is earned normally.

**5. Redemption**

The angel who goes out may erase their largest negative score from either of the first two rounds.

If they have no previous negative score, they receive no additional benefit.

### 10. Scoring

A round ends when an angel empties their hand.

Each remaining angel receives negative points equal to the total face value of their undelivered souls.

The angel who goes out receives positive points equal to the sum of the other angels' penalties.

The active Final Judgment decree may modify individual scores.

Scores accumulate across all three rounds.

The angel with the highest cumulative score wins and becomes God's Favorite.

### 11. Playtest Rulings Still Needed

The following details remain provisional or unresolved:

1. **Sanctuary:** Whether a protected formation may be released or replaced freely after the power has been used.
*it may be released freely, but not replaced.*

2. **Purgatory:** Whether a soul still in Purgatory counts as an undelivered soul when the round ends.
*it was counted when it entered, and remains counted by that angel regardless*

3. **Jokers:** Exact recovery rules, especially how exchanging a joker affects Divine Favor.
*you may replace a joker with a soul from your hand that is legal in its place,
but this doesn't count as delivering a new soul (net zero effect)* 

4. **Mirror Choir:** The precise legal combinations of complementary ranks.
*specified above*

5. **Decree interpretation:** Whether each decree replaces the relevant ordinary formation rule or adds another permitted formation.
*decrees replace formations*

6. **Divine Favor:** How to prevent retrieving and replaying the same souls from artificially increasing lifetime contributions.
*I don't understand the question*

7. **Special scoring:** Whether catch-up credits alter only the recipient's score or also the winner's positive score.
*I don't understand the question*

8. **Bag exhaustion:** Follow standard Rummikub stalemate rules unless playtesting suggests a change.
*yes*

These should be settled before the affected mechanic is used in a formal test.

### 12. What to Observe During Playtesting

The first playtests should focus on:

- Whether Divine Favor discourages hoarding.
- Whether the no-threshold opening makes the shared tableau develop quickly.
- Whether angels' powers create interesting decisions rather than automatic advantages.
- Whether all formations remain easy to verify.
- Whether the ordinary decrees genuinely change how players solve the puzzle.
- Whether the Final Judgment gives trailing players realistic comeback opportunities.
- Whether three rounds produce an enjoyable game length.

The goal is to preserve Rummikub's combinatorial depth while making every round feel different, rewarding productive contributions, and creating memorable interactions between the angels.

*Heaven is a team effort. Unfortunately, only one angel gets promoted.*
